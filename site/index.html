The operating model

Freelancers operate on-platform chatbots and subcontract bounded tasks to other professionals. Teams grow or shrink with each project's needs, combining human judgment with supervised AI assistance.

The proposal connects prompt intake, tenant workspaces, Tether-bound sessions, contractual milestones, completion evidence, API credits and payment monitoring.

A concrete example

A business needs a support workflow. Its operator recruits integration and content specialists, uses AI assistants for approved tasks and brings in a reviewer. The customer inspects evidence and accepts the deliverable before settlement.

The documents include an illustrative budget. Rates and fees are assumptions, not quotations or validated platform economics.

Evidence and status

The current implementation is a PHP/JSON marketplace. The constellation workflow and live integrations are proposed capabilities. These documents do not claim an operating network, customer traction or proven savings.
